# 6 - Floating meetings

The meetings panel should be on a fixed position, with two modes: full-screen and mini-player.

The user may toggle between them using a button on the top-right of the panel.

On mini-player the panel may be freely dragged along the screen by holding its header, but on drop it will move on its own to the closest of these positions:

top-left top-center top-right
mid-left            mid-right
bot-left bot-center bot-right


The meetings panel should include the chat as a column to the right, may be toggled to be hidden or visible.
