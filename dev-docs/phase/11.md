# 11 - Unified Notifications

Lets create a unified notifications system.

## Visually

A column with transparent background that overlays the content on the right of the view; scrollable if necessary. Above all else, including the meeting.
Its height fills in all available space of the content view, not including the navbar.

in it, each notification gets its own bubble, with optionally buttons and an x to close it.

## Funcionally

Content loaded on startup that may appear as notifications (like org invites) are placed there.
Events like meeting invites or meeting join requests are placed there.
